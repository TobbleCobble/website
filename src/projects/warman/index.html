Project Case Study

Warman Bot

Overview

The Warman Competition is a prestigious engineering challenge. My role involved the design and fabrication of an autonomous robot designed to collect rocks and transport them across a complex terrain.

The standout feature of this build was the rack and pinion dual actuator system, which allowed the robot to reach out over double its length to collect and then deposit rocks.

Technologies Used

Arduino Mega Mechatronics Rack & Pinion CAD Design

Key Outcomes

  • ● 1m Vertical Displacement
  • ● Precision Rock Collection
  • ● Dual Actuator Integration
